## General Tourism Information for Sri Lanka

**Overview**
Sri Lanka is an island nation in South Asia known for its diverse landscapes, rich cultural heritage, and warm hospitality. With its tropical climate, beautiful beaches, ancient cities, and abundant wildlife, it offers a wide range of experiences for tourists.

**Key Tourist Destinations**

1. **Cultural Triangle** - Includes ancient cities of Anuradhapura, Polonnaruwa, and Sigiriya (Lion Rock fortress)
2. **Kandy** - Home to the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic and the Kandy Esala Perahera festival
3. **Galle** - Colonial-era fort city with Dutch architecture, cobblestone streets, and boutique shops
4. **Ella** - Picturesque hill country town known for hiking, tea plantations, and the Nine Arch Bridge
5. **Yala National Park** - Famous for leopard sightings and diverse wildlife
6. **Beaches** - Mirissa, Unawatuna, Bentota, Arugam Bay (for surfing)

**Cultural Experiences**
- Traditional Kandyan dance performances
- Ayurvedic wellness treatments and spas
- Tea plantation tours in the central highlands
- Buddhist temples and religious ceremonies
- Local cooking classes featuring Sri Lankan cuisine

**Practical Information**
- Best visiting season: December to March for the west and south; May to September for the east coast
- Currency: Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR)
- Languages: Sinhala and Tamil (official), English widely spoken in tourist areas
- Transportation: Tuk-tuks, buses, trains, and domestic flights
- Visa requirements: Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) available online for most nationalities
